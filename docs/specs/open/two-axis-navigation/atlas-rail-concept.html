Concept Atlas — the decisions

Companion to atlas-rail-concept.md. Every claim below is demonstrated live — click the map, collapse the rail, follow an escalation.

1
Orientation moves to the edge; the canvas is never overlaid.The Meridian capsule floated over the work. Atlas anchors chrome to the left edge and the top strip — Compare's split panes and Benchmark's matrix get every pixel of canvas, at every scroll position.concept §A.1 · §C
2
The 2×2 is a map, not two dials.Subjects are rows, scale is columns, cells are links. You recognize your destination — "test models across my task set" — instead of computing subject × scope. The taxonomy stays visible and self-teaching; it just stops being a control. Links use standard nav semantics, not radiogroups.concept §A.2 · §C.1
3
One click to any cell; you always know where you are.Compare sits top-left — the home position. The active cell carries an accent bar, weight, and a dot (never hue alone). The eyebrow restates the axis on every surface, so wayfinding survives even with the rail collapsed.concept §C.1 · spec §K law
4
Cross-cell movement is escalation, on the surface, in context."Calibrate finish policies on this task", "Create a Task Set from this task", "Test across a Task Set", "Run a Policy Study on this Task Set" — the carry semantics the spec already defines (F2–F5), rendered as actions where the work lives. Nothing flips an axis in chrome.concept §B · spec §F
5
Finish never leaves Compare.Rank/Fuse is an execution setting, so it renders inside the Compare surface only — never in the rail, never in the strip. In Strategies, the same words are the experiment itself: rows in a table, never a selector.spec §C.2b
6
The pool is always on stage in Strategies.A strategy test is a treatment over a pinned model pool. Calibrate and Policy Study both pin it visibly — pool chips, judge, rubric, "held constant across every policy."spec §C.2a
7
Calibrate wears its evidence grade on its sleeve.One task yields a compass reading, so the surface says exactly that — snapshot banner, per-policy table, zero confidence theater. Its single call to action is escalation.spec §G.3.3–G.3.4
8
The rail collapses to a column of icons — order preserved.Press [ or the chevron: labels fold away and the four cells stay in map order as 44px icon targets. Tooltips and accessible names carry the full descriptors, and the eyebrow keeps stating the axis on the surface. State persists per session.concept §C.2
9
Mobile: drawer + thumb-zone bar.Below 780px the rail becomes a drawer from the leading edge; the four testing cells live in a bottom bar with safe-area padding. The strip keeps awareness (live run, search, attention) — never navigation.concept §C.2 · §E.4
10
What this concept deliberately omits.No axis controls anywhere. No floating chrome. No global analytics. Observatory and Records stay library utilities — grouped subordinate to Testing, never peers of the cells. One accent color; mono + tabular numerals for every number; motion only where it communicates state.concept §D–E · spec §I
