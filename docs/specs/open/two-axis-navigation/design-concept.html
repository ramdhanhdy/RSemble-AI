Concept Meridian — the decisions

Companion to two-axis-navigation-spec.md. Click the dials — every claim below is demonstrated live.

1
Two dials, one rule.Navigation asks the only two questions that matter: what varies (subject) and at what scale (scope). Any workflow — seen or unseen — has a computable home. The old Lab/Evaluations confusion was category overlap; dials don't overlap.spec §C.1
2
The cluster floats.A translucent capsule separates orientation from content — material hierarchy instead of painted bars. It persists across every surface; the dials never move, so your hands learn them.spec §E · materials: WWDC fluid interfaces
3
Surfaces lead with verdicts, not chrome.The eyebrow locates you (Models × Single); the headline is the content — the task, the set, the question. No redundant page furniture repeating what the dials already say.wayfinding: answer where am I with content
4
The pool is always on stage in Strategies.A strategy test is a treatment applied over a pinned model pool — hiding the pool would make the dials lie. In Calibrate and Policy Study it's pinned, visible, subordinate.spec §C.2a
5
Calibrate wears its evidence grade on its sleeve.One task yields a compass reading, so the surface says exactly that — snapshot banner, per-policy table, zero confidence theater. Its single call to action is escalation: earn a study in one tap.spec §G.3.3 – G.3.4
6
Finish never leaves Compare.Rank/Fuse is an execution setting, so it renders inside the Compare surface only. In Strategies, the same words are the experiment itself — rows in a table, never a selector.spec §C.2b (strengthens Child 08 §C.3)
7
Numbers are instruments.Mono + tabular numerals for every score, cost, and timer — nothing jitters while values tick. Hierarchy comes from weight and position; color is paired with dots and glyphs, never hue alone.type: WWDC details of UI typography
8
Motion is critically damped and interruptible.Dial thumbs slide on the drawer curve; surfaces cross-fade through a 3px blur mask; every press lands at scale(0.97). All of it collapses to a gentle fade under prefers-reduced-motion.damping 1.0, response 0.3–0.4 · reduced-motion variant
9
What this concept deliberately omits.The Observatory, Records, Attention, and Search never become dials — they live in the utility cluster because they answer different questions (what do I know / what happened / what's broken). One accent color. No gradients on text. No decoration pretending to be state.spec §I
